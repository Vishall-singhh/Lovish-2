CURSOR
- Custom cursor and liquid ribbon are enabled on desktop devices with a fine pointer.
- Mobile and touch devices use the standard pointer.
- The ribbon uses a tapered, interpolated fluid trail with graphite body, pearl highlights and crimson refraction.
- Click produces a short ripple; there are no persistent particle dots.
